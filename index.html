Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the enrollment and learning process.

What is the weekly time commitment?

This is an intensive track. You should budget 10-15 hours per week for live sessions, self-paced study, and lab work. Consistency is the key to mastering the architectural patterns we teach.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. These modules are designed for transitioning beginners and walk them straight into Enterprise patterns and Production-grade architecture.

How do the payment plans work?

We offer a 3-installment plan. You pay a deposit to secure your seat, followed by two monthly installments.

What if I miss a live session?

Life happens. All sessions are recorded and uploaded to the student portal within 24 hours, along with all repository links, documentation, and the session's specific coding assignment.

Will I get a certificate at the end?

Upon successful completion of your Capstone project and passing the final technical review, you will receive a Certificate of Engineering Excellence.
